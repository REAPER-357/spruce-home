How it works

Your code is stretched into two secrets: a hidden room address and an AES-256 key. The key never leaves your device.

Messages and photos are encrypted with that key before they are sent. Calls use WebRTC's built-in encryption.

A free public broker only helps the two devices find each other. It sees a random-looking room ID and IP addresses, never your messages.

Nothing is saved. Closing the chat erases the conversation.
